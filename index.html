Dog Day

One day, sunrise to dusk. Read the day's story, follow the treasure map, and get your dog to bed happy.

🐶 How to play Dog Day

A dog-care game where you read a story and solve riddles to find a treasure.

👧🧒 For kids

  • 🐶Pick your dog.
  • ☀️You have one day, from morning until night.
  • 🎾Tap the buttons to play, eat, nap and go places.
  • 📊Watch the bars: Energy, Happy, Full, Clean. Keep them up!
  • 📖Read today's story all by yourself. Hard word? Tap it to hear it. You only get 5 listens!
  • 🔊Riddles too: tap a word or read the riddle. You can hear the answers too. You get 4 listens for the whole hunt.
  • 🧰Solve the riddles. They tell you where the treasure is. Go there and sniff!
  • 🚽Dogs need to go to the bathroom outside. Watch the Potty bar!
  • ⛈️Look at the weather. Dogs do not like storms!
  • 🌦️Tap the weather to read about it. Answer the questions for points!
  • 🐾At night your dog goes home to his family. More points = more puppies (up to 5)!

👩👨 For parents

What it is. A gentle dog-care game with reading practice at a 1st-grade level. One day in the game takes about 15–25 minutes. You can't lose: the day always ends with a score.

Reading. Each day has one story (10 short sentences over 5 pages) and 3 questions about it. Your child can hear a word by tapping it, or hear the whole page, but only 5 times per story, so they try to sound words out first. Skipping the story doesn't work: it comes back as the bedtime story. Stars are for answers right on the first try.

Treasure hunt. 5 riddles, each with 4 answers. Your child can hear a word, the whole riddle or an answer, but only 4 times in the whole hunt. The answer is a clue to a place in the game, so your child has to read and think before travelling there.

Weather reading. Tapping the weather opens a short article about it with 2 questions. Right answers earn points, once per weather type each day.

Planning. Every action takes time and changes the dog's needs. A good day balances playing and resting, and plans around the weather forecast.

Tips. Let your child read the story out loud to you. Ask "what does your dog need now?" Turn the sound up: the device's voice reads the words.

Privacy. No account, no ads and no internet needed after loading. Nothing is saved: every reload is a new day.
