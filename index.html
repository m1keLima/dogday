🐶 How to play Dog Day

A dog-care game where you read a story and solve riddles to find a treasure.

👧🧒 For kids

  • 🐶Pick your grade, a theme and your dog.
  • ☀️You have one day, from morning until night.
  • 🎾Tap the buttons to play, eat, nap and go places.
  • 📊Watch the bars: Energy, Happy, Food, Water, Clean, Potty. Keep them all up!
  • 📖Read today's story all by yourself. Hard word? Tap it to hear it. Each page gives you 1 listen — save them up if you don't need them!
  • 🔊Riddles too: tap a word or read the riddle. You can hear the answers too. Each riddle gives you 1 listen, and you can save them.
  • 🧰Solve the riddles. They tell you where the treasure is. Go there and sniff!
  • 🚽Dogs go to the bathroom outside. When the Potty bar gets low, take him out!
  • ⛈️Look at the weather. Dogs do not like storms!
  • 🌦️Tap the weather to read about it. Answer the questions for points!
  • 🐾At night your dog goes home to his family. More points = more puppies (up to 5)! But no reading stars (story and weather) = no puppies.

👩👨 For parents

What it is. A gentle dog-care game with reading practice at a 1st-grade level. One day in the game takes about 15–25 minutes. You can't lose: the day always ends with a score.

Reading. Each day has one story (10 short sentences over 5 pages) and 3 questions about it. Your child can hear a word by tapping it, or hear the whole page, earning 1 listen per page; unused listens carry over to later pages and the questions, so there's a reason to try sounding words out first. Skipping the story doesn't work: it comes back as the bedtime story. Every page read earns a ⭐ and every question answered earns a ⭐. Stopping part-way keeps the stars already earned, and reopening carries on from where your child stopped.

Treasure hunt. 5 riddles, each with 4 answers. Your child can hear a word, the whole riddle or an answer, earning 1 listen per riddle, saved up if unused. The answer is a clue to a place in the game, so your child has to read and think before travelling there.

Weather reading. Tapping the weather opens a short article about it with 2 questions. It earns 1 listen per page (3 in all), saved up if unused. Right answers earn points, once per weather type each day. Puppies need reading: at night the puppies only come out if your child earned at least one star on the story and one on a weather article.

Reading level & theme. On the first screen pick a grade from K to 5th. Each grade has its own stories, and pages get longer as the grade goes up (from 2 tiny sentences in K to a full paragraph in 5th). Grades 3–5 get 4 questions (main idea, vocabulary, inference) and harder riddles and science-based weather articles. Pick a theme too: any of the 4 seasons or any holiday (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Befana, Valentine's, Mardi Gras, St. Patrick's, Easter, Earth Day, Cinco de Mayo, Memorial Day, 4th of July, Ferragosto), each with its own decorations, dog outfit, weather and matching stories. While a holiday is on (for example 10 Oct – 2 Nov for Halloween) it's picked for you; otherwise the current season is. Each holiday has a treat: the dog-safe ones (🥚 🍎 🍓 🍉) go to the dog, and for the others (like 🍫 or 🍬) the game explains why they're not for dogs and gives him a 🦴.

Planning. Every action takes time and changes the dog's needs. A good day balances playing and resting, and plans around the weather forecast.

Tips. Let your child read the story out loud to you. Ask "what does your dog need now?" Turn the sound up: the device's voice reads the words.

Privacy. No account, no ads and no internet needed after loading. Nothing is saved: every reload is a new day.
